=====================================================================
LIYA PY PORTFOLIO — IMAGES FOLDER
=====================================================================

Drop your custom image files here!

RECOMMENDED FILES:
1. `liya.jpg` - Your profile photo for the hero center card
2. `resume.pdf` - Put your resume in the `public/` folder as `Liya_Py_Resume.pdf`
3. `project-1.jpg`, `project-2.jpg`, etc. - Custom screenshots of your apps

To use your local photo:
1. Copy your photo here: `public/images/liya.jpg`
2. Open `src/data.js`
3. In `hero.cards`, update the profile card image:
   image: "/images/liya.jpg"
